# Sprint 7B — Topical Authority & SERP Outranking Design

Date: 2026-08-08
Branch: `seo/normandie-v2`

## 1. Objective

Build four premium editorial assets that strengthen Tourma-Line's topical authority without recreating doorway pages. Each article must have a unique search intent, a clear internal-link destination, and a measurable role in the architecture.

The standard is not "publish useful content". The standard is: study what currently ranks, identify the dominant keyword/topic coverage and user expectations, then publish a substantially more complete, clearer, more trustworthy and better-connected answer without copying competitors.

## 2. Constraints

- No fake local presence in Rouen, Le Havre or Dieppe.
- No city-template article variants.
- No duplicated intent with existing `Chemin de Vie`, `Mission d'âme`, Lahochi-distance or cartomancie-phone content.
- No medical claims or guaranteed predictive outcomes.
- No unsupported "best", "most reliable" or similar superiority claims.
- Cabinet identity remains Gerponville; remote consultation may be mentioned where true.
- Content should support user decisions and free will rather than create dependency.

## 3. Competitive SERP findings

### 3.1 Choosing a numerologist

Current ranking pages repeatedly cover:
- experience/reputation;
- method transparency;
- personalization;
- reviews/testimonials;
- tariff clarity;
- duration and mode of consultation;
- ethics, limits and confidentiality;
- what information the client must provide.

Weakness observed: many pages are generic, marketplace-led or focused on selling a platform rather than helping a reader compare a practitioner rigorously.

Tourma-Line opportunity: publish a neutral decision framework with a practical checklist, questions to ask before booking, red flags, difference between symbolic interpretation and prediction, what a real consultation includes, and a transparent Tourma-Line example only after the neutral framework.

### 3.2 Personal year in numerology

The strongest current pages usually include:
- direct definition;
- calculation formula;
- worked examples;
- interpretation of years 1–9;
- universal year 2026;
- personal year vs life path;
- FAQ;
- calculator or interactive element on some competitors.

There is a visible methodological disagreement in current content: some sources describe the cycle as calendar-year based, others as birthday-to-birthday. This must not be silently presented as a single undisputed rule.

Tourma-Line opportunity: explain both conventions, state the method used by Line/Tourma-Line, show multiple worked examples, give a compact 1–9 interpretation table, explain how the personal year interacts with life path, and link to the existing Chemin de Vie guide instead of duplicating it.

### 3.3 Choosing a serious fortune teller / cartomancer

Current ranking pages repeatedly cover:
- transparent prices;
- identifiable practitioner;
- verifiable reviews;
- years of practice;
- no guaranteed result;
- no pressure or fear tactics;
- respect for free will;
- confidentiality;
- clear modality (cabinet, phone, remote);
- warning signs such as repeated requests for money, guaranteed return of a partner, or dependency-building behavior.

Weakness observed: much of the SERP is self-promotional, repetitive and vague about how readers can verify claims independently.

Tourma-Line opportunity: create the strongest practical checklist in the SERP, with "green flags", "red flags", how to verify an identity/business, how to read reviews critically, what to ask before paying, how to compare forfait vs per-minute pricing, and when not to consult.

### 3.4 Where to consult in Seine-Maritime

Current territorial SERPs are dominated by directories and local practitioners. PagesJaunes exposes around 35 voyance/cartomancie results for Seine-Maritime, while local Rouen listings contain established practitioners with physical addresses and many reviews.

Weakness observed: directory pages list providers but do not explain which consultation mode suits which situation, how geography should influence the choice, or how to compare proximity vs remote consultation.

Tourma-Line opportunity: create an editorial guide that explains the actual Tourma-Line geography honestly: Gerponville cabinet, nearby validated local clusters, published hubs, and distance consultation for people farther away. It must link to hubs rather than create faux-local city articles.

## 5. Outranking framework

Every article must beat the current SERP on at least six dimensions:

1. **Intent completeness** — answer the main question immediately, then cover the adjacent questions users need next.
2. **Information gain** — include a decision framework, checklist, comparison table, worked example, or methodology clarification not consistently present in competing pages.
3. **Trust** — identifiable author, real business identity, transparent limits, no guaranteed claims, clear pricing links when available.
4. **Structure** — concise title, descriptive H1, scannable H2/H3, summary box/table, practical FAQ.
5. **Internal authority flow** — article → service pillar → hub/local validated page → booking; and reciprocal contextual links from relevant pillar/hub pages.
6. **Freshness and maintainability** — avoid unnecessary years in evergreen titles unless the year is central to intent; where 2026 matters, make the article easy to update annually.
7. **Mobile UX** — short opening, compact tables, no walls of text, buttons large enough, no aggressive interstitials.
8. **Entity clarity** — Line Simon / Tourma-Line / Gerponville / service relationships must match the site-wide schema and visible content.

## 6. SEO implementation requirements

For each article:
- unique slug, title, `seoTitle`, meta description, author and publication/update date;
- one canonical URL;
- included in the normal blog routing and sitemap;
- `Article`/`BlogPosting` structured data only where supported by visible page data;
- descriptive breadcrumb;
- 3–6 contextual internal links;
- one primary CTA and one optional secondary CTA maximum;
- no keyword-stuffed city lists;
- no duplicated FAQ across the four articles;
- no FAQ rich-result strategy dependency;
- no separate pages for query variants.

## 7. Competitive research workflow before drafting

Before writing each article, rerun current SERP research for its primary query and at least 3 secondary queries. Record:
- top organic page types;
- repeated terms and subtopics;
- common H2/H3 patterns;
- tables/calculators/checklists used;
- trust signals;
- gaps or contradictions;
- local-vs-national intent split;
- freshness dates where visible.

The draft must then intentionally fill the gaps rather than merely become longer.

## 8. Testing and acceptance criteria

A future `verify-editorial-authority.mjs` should fail if:
- any of the 4 approved articles is missing;
- article slugs collide with existing content;
- Article A lacks links to `/numerologie` and the Chemin de Vie cluster;
- Article B omits the methodological convention distinction;
- Article C lacks explicit anti-pressure / no-guarantee guidance;
- Article D claims a physical office outside Gerponville;
- any article creates or links to a new Rouen/Le Havre/Dieppe city landing contrary to Sprint 7A HOLD decisions;
- any article contains prohibited medical/guaranteed-result language;
- the four articles are absent from generated routes/sitemap after publication.

Build/verification must return GREEN before the PR can leave draft.

## 9. Non-goals

- No 6-article expansion in this sprint.
- No new Rouen, Le Havre or Dieppe commercial landing.
- No automatic publishing based on keyword count.
- No promise of #1 ranking.
- No copying competitor wording or article structure verbatim.

## 10. Success metrics after deployment

Track at J+14 and J+28:
- indexing status;
- impressions and clicks by article;
- new query families;
- CTR;
- average position trend;
- assisted clicks toward `/numerologie`, `/cartomancie`, published hubs and booking;
- whether Rouen/Le Havre/Dieppe informational queries begin surfacing without a fake local landing.

A content winner should be strengthened with additional internal links or updates; a page with impressions but weak CTR should receive title/meta testing; a page with no meaningful signals after sufficient crawl time should be reviewed rather than multiplied into variants.
